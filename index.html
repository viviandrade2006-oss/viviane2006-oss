Why I Wrote This Book

My journey began with early menopause and unexpected physical and emotional changes. I had questions, but finding clear guidance was difficult. So I began researching menopause and women’s health.

The Butterfly Menopause grew from that experience. This guide brings together what I learned through my own journey with educational, science-backed information — so other women can feel more informed and supported as they navigate theirs.
